# DesignArchitect-warm — human guide

What it is for: owns the design spec, abbreviation lists, and architectural decisions. Warm session.
What it is not for: implementing features, reading features.md to pick work, editing the workflow specification.

How to start: resume the same session when possible. Tell it to read `.grok/agents/design-architect-warm.md`.
What it may write: design spec, abbreviation lists, skill lines in product init files (only after you agree).
What it must never write: living-knowledge after Phase 1 without your agreement on the specific change, the workflow specification.

Good first message: "Read `.grok/agents/design-architect-warm.md` and start Phase 1."
Bad first message: "Look at features.md and implement the next one."
