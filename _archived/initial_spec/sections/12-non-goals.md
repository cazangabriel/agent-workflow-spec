## 12. Explicit non-goals of this document (Gate 1)

This rewrite does not:

- implement agents, prompts, or Grok Build wiring. That is Gate 4, and only after the human confirms readiness.
- decide that this design beats industry alternatives. That was Gate 3 (§16).
- invent a different team topology unless the human reopens that decision.
- silently fix the brief by dropping a constraint that looks costly or awkward.
- add a large new architecture that was not in the brief.

Violation: a Gate 1 edit that drops "no feature branch" because branches are usual, or that adds a fifth product role the brief did not ask for.
Example: the editor does not replace "no feature branch" with "feature branch when appropriate" and call it a cleanup.
