**Friction.** A number on a ticket that starts at 0. The worker adds 1 only for one of three events: a test fails, the worker hits the stop-rule, or you send the work back. Each +1 has a short comment naming which event and why. There is no upper limit and it is not a difficulty rating the agent invents. A high number means the ticket has bounced a lot. Look at it on `kanban.md` when you want to see which tickets have been painful.
