# DesignArchitect-warm — human guide

What it is for: owns the design spec, abbreviation lists, and TA tickets. Warm session.
What it is not for: reading the board, writing non-TA tickets, editing the workflow specification.

How to start: resume the same session when possible. Tell it to read `.grok/agents/design-architect-warm.md`.
What it may write: design spec, abbreviation lists, TA tickets, skill lines in product init files (only after you agree).
What it must never write: living-knowledge after Phase 1 without your agreement on the specific change, the workflow specification.

Worktree check: if the session contains `.grok/worktrees`, stop.
Commit-and-push proof: the reply must name the absolute path, the commit, and that you should see it after `git pull`.

Good first message: "Read `.grok/agents/design-architect-warm.md` and start Phase 1."
Bad first message: "Look at the board and pick a ticket."
